# SecretChecklist

WoW is full of secrets that the game never tells you about — hidden mounts, pets, toys, achievements, appearances and housing decor, found by the community and documented nowhere in-game.

SecretChecklist keeps track of them for you. It knows what you've already collected the moment you log in, and shows you exactly what's left.

## What it does

**Shows you where you stand.** Every secret in one Collections-style window, coloured when you have it, greyed when you don't.

**Walks you through the ones you're missing.** Every secret has a step-by-step guide, and steps tick themselves off as you go. Click a step with a location and it drops a map pin.

**Finds anything fast.** Search by name, by zone, or by anything mentioned in the steps — "Kosumoth" and "Tazavesh" both work.

**Shows you the reward** in 3D before you go after it, including appearances on your own character.

**Tells you when you get one**, with a toast the moment it registers.

## Doing the Beledar raid?

The Radiant Singer secret needs 40 players performing emotes in sequence at the Divine Flame. SecretChecklist gets you your slot and your emote automatically — **only the raid leader needs the BeledarOrchestra addon**, everyone else just needs this.

## Also

- Matches your UI: Default, ElvUI or EllesmereUI
- Collected and missing use a checkmark and a cross, not just colour
- Available in 11 languages
- Minimap button, or `/secrets`
